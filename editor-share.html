The Hidden Greek Islands

Skip Mykonos. Seven islands every travel agent should be quietly recommending instead.

Greece has 227 inhabited islands. Most of your clients can name three. The fix isn't fighting harder for Santorini bookings — it's offering them somewhere they haven't seen on Instagram yet.

This guide rounds up the seven islands worth a serious look this summer. Each one has direct flights from a UK airport, hotels at every price point, and enough local character that your client will come home thanking you for the recommendation.

Folegandros is Cycladic, whitewashed, dramatically clifftop and almost entirely unspoilt. Two hours by ferry from Santorini, but it might as well be two decades earlier.

Tinos is the Greeks' favourite Greek island. Cycladic but with mountains, marble villages and food that punches well above the standard taverna fare.

Each of these islands books at higher margins because clients aren't price-comparing them on the OTAs. Send them to Folegandros and they'll send you their friends.
