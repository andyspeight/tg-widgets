The Hidden Greek Islands

Skip Mykonos. Seven islands every travel agent should be quietly recommending instead.

Greece has 227 inhabited islands. Most of your clients can name three. The fix isn't fighting harder for Santorini bookings, it's offering them somewhere they haven't seen on Instagram yet.

Folegandros is Cycladic, whitewashed and dramatically clifftop. Two hours from Santorini but two decades earlier in feel.

Each of these islands books at higher margins because clients aren't price-comparing them on the OTAs. Send them to Folegandros and they'll send you their friends.
