Day by day — sample

Jotunheimen Traverse

Gjendesheim to Turtagrø · Norway · hut to hut

92 kmDistance
2,469 mHigh point
6 daysDuration
  1. D1

    Gjendesheim & the Besseggen ridge

    Straight onto the traverse's most famous line — the arête between green Gjende and blue Bessvatnet. Hands out of pockets for twenty minutes of easy scrambling.

    14 km6–7 hrs
  2. D2

    Memurubu to Gjendebu

    High shoulder above the lake then a long descent through birch. Shorter day; your legs will thank you by dinner.

    10 km4–5 hrs
  3. D3

    Into the Svartdalen valley

    The quiet heart of the range — a stone valley between glaciers, usually shared with nobody but reindeer.

    16 km7 hrs
  4. D4

    Leirvassbu & Kyrkja

    Optional afternoon ascent of Kyrkja, the "church" — an honest 2,032m pyramid with a scrambly top. Skippable without shame.

    12 km5–8 hrs
  5. D5

    Over the high col

    The traverse's crux day: 2,469m, snow patches into July, and weather that changes its mind hourly. We set off early and carry storm kit.

    19 km8–9 hrs
  6. D6

    Descent to Turtagrø

    Down past the Skagastølstindane wall to the old climbers' hotel. Showers, a long table, and the traverse behind you.

    21 km7 hrs

Straight talk: this is a Grade 3 — Tough. You need to be comfortable walking 6–8 hours on rough ground, several days running, carrying 10–12kg. If that sounds like a stretch, ring us — we'll point you at the right trip instead of selling you the wrong one.

The grading system

How hard is hard?

Five grades, applied honestly. We'd rather lose a booking than watch you have a miserable week at altitude.

1Steady

Full days out, good paths, no exposure. Fit enough to enjoy a long hilly weekend and you're fine.

e.g. Slovenia's Julian Foothills7 days · from £1,240

2Solid

Bigger days or new skills — a rope, a paddle, a heavier pack. Regular hill walkers and gym-fit beginners do well here.

e.g. Railay Climbing Week8 days · from £1,690

3Tough

6–8 hour days on rough ground, back to back, sometimes at altitude. Train for it and it'll be the best week of your year.

e.g. Annapurna Sanctuary14 days · from £2,865

4Hard

Long days, real weather, cumulative fatigue. You'll dig in at least once. This is the grade most of our regulars come back for.

e.g. Walker's Haute Route12 days · from £2,340

5Savage

Expedition conditions: glacier travel, nights above 5,000m, summit days that start at 1am. We interview before we confirm your place.

e.g. Mera Peak, 6,476m16 days · from £3,940

Every booking includes a 15-minute call with your trip leader before you commit to a Grade 4 or 5. We move roughly one in eight enquirers to a different grade — in both directions.
