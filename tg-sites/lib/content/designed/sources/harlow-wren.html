Tell us where you are in the world

The itinerary is the last thing we make.

Most of the work happens before anything is booked, in conversation and in the years of notes our designers keep on the houses, the rooms and the people they know by name.

  1. I

    A conversation, not a form

    An hour with your designer, by telephone or at the studio. How you like to travel, what you never want to do again, who is coming, and what the journey is for. Nothing is booked, nothing is sold.

  2. II

    One proposal, fully priced

    Within a week, a single considered journey, not three padded for choice, with every cost written down to the transfers. We revise it with you until it is right, however many rounds that takes.

  3. III

    A hand on it while you travel

    Your designer, reachable on a direct line day and night. When fog closed Florence last November, our travellers were driven to Bologna and away on the fast train before the airport had finished apologising.

You deal with one person, start to finish.

Harlow & Wren is nine designers above a bookbinder's in Fitzrovia. Each takes a small book of travellers, keeps to the regions they know in their bones, and answers their own telephone. The person who plans your journey is the person who picks up when you call from the road.

This month your first conversation is likely to be with Cordelia Wren, who has spent nineteen Septembers driving the back roads of Tuscany and Umbria, checking rooms.
