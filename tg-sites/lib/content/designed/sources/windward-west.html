When to go — the honest version

June to November is hurricane season. There — we said it.

Most agents whisper it. We'd rather you knew: the season is real, and most weeks inside it are still glorious. Storms are tracked days out, direct hits on any single island are rare, and the green season means fuller waterfalls, quieter beaches and prices 25–40% lower.

Two more things the brochures skip: Barbados, Grenada and Tobago sit at the very southern edge of the belt and are struck far less often. And if you travel with us in season, we book flexible rates and you're fully ATOL-protected either way.

Our promise: if we wouldn't take our own family that week, we'll tell you — and move you. Twice in twenty years we've rung clients and said "not this fortnight". Both still book with us.
