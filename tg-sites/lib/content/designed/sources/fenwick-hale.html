Led by scholars, not reps.

Twenty-eight lecturers travel with us. They are chosen for two things: depth of knowledge, and the rarer gift of wearing it lightly.

Dr Eleanor Vasey

Byzantine historian · University of Birmingham (em.)

Author of The City and the Sea: Constantinople 330–1453. Eleanor has led our Byzantine tours since 2009 and maintains that the best lecture theatre in the world is the gallery of Hagia Sophia at eight in the morning.

Section03

A tour in detail: A Venetian Winter

Tour No. 14, ten nights, exactly as the brochure prints it. Itineraries are published in full before you book — nothing is a surprise, including the free afternoons.

Day1

Arrive by water

Afternoon flight, launch to the hotel, and a first walk to the Salute before dinner together.

Day2

Torcello, before the boats fill

Private launch at 8.40am to the cathedral's Last Judgement. Lunch at a locanda; Burano optional.

Day3

The Accademia, slowly

A full morning with the Bellinis — one gallery, properly, rather than three at a trot.

Day4

San Giorgio & a free evening

The Palladian church and the Cini cloisters; the concierge holds concert tickets for those who want them.

Day5

The Doge's Venice

Palazzo Ducale by the quiet entrance, then Dr Marchetti's talk, Venice and the Sea, before dinner.

Day6

A day that is yours

Unplanned by design. Suggestions in writing for three walks, two churches and one very good bacaro.

Day7

Ravenna, by coach

The sixth-century mosaics of San Vitale and Galla Placidia — under two hours each way, lunch included.

Day8

The Ghetto & Cannaregio

Five hundred years of the Ghetto Nuovo, then Madonna dell'Orto and Tintoretto's parish.

Day9

Carnival, at arm's length

The Piazza in mask season from a first-floor window, with coffee — in it, but not of it.

Day10

A last morning, unhurried

Nothing before eleven. Cases travel ahead; a farewell lunch near the Rialto, then the launch out.

The printed itinerary runs to four pages, including both hotels by name, every included meal, and a walking note for each day. Request it by post →

On pace

No hotel is kept for fewer than two nights. Cases travel door to door without you lifting them. Mornings start civilly, coach stages stay short, and every day contains at least one hour that belongs entirely to you.

Where a site involves steep ground or long standing, the itinerary says so in plain terms — and offers an alternative.

Section04

Included means included.

  • Always includedReturn flights from London (regional connections arranged), all excursions and entries, most meals with wine, tips, and the lecturer's company throughout.
  • Never includedNothing hidden — the only regular extras are travel insurance and the meals we deliberately leave free, listed on each tour page.
  • HotelsQuiet, central, and chosen on foot by our own staff — usually four-star, always the sort of place you would pick yourself. Named in the brochure, every one.
  • The groupBetween 10 and 22 guests, most of them travelling with us for the third time or more. A tour manager accompanies every departure alongside the lecturer.

Travelling on your own?

You will be in good company — around a third of our guests do. Dinner is at shared tables from the first evening, rooms are proper doubles for sole use, and a growing number of departures carry no single supplement at all.

“I booked the Peloponnese tour alone with some trepidation. By Nafplio I had a table of friends, and by Olympia we were planning next year's tour together.”

Margaret Holloway, Ludlow — three tours since 2023
