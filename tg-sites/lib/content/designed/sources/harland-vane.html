Tell us where you are in the world

The itinerary is the last thing we produce.

Most of the work happens before anything is booked — in conversation, and in the twenty years of notes, room numbers and named contacts our designers keep on the places they cover.

  1. 01

    A conversation, not a form

    An hour with your designer, by phone or at the studio. How you like to travel, what you never want to do again, who is coming, what the trip is for. Nothing is booked, nothing is sold.

  2. 02

    One proposal, fully priced

    Within a week: a single considered itinerary — not three options padded for choice — with every cost stated, down to the transfers. We revise it with you until it is right, however many rounds that takes.

  3. 03

    While you travel

    Your designer, reachable on a direct line, day and night. When the fog closed Venice’s airport last November, our clients were re-routed through Verona with a car waiting before their gondola reached the hotel.
