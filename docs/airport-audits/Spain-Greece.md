# Spain and Greece: entry-rules re-verification

40 records audited 25 August 2026 (20 Spain, 20 Greece). Prior Verified Date on
every one: 11 May 2026. A further 7 live records in Portugal and Cyprus carried
the same error and were corrected in the same pass.

## The systemic error: ETIAS

**Every one of the 40 records said "ETIAS pre-travel authorisation expected Q4
2026, €20 fee, mandatory for UK travellers."**

That is withdrawn guidance. The EU removed the late-2026 target from the
official ETIAS site in mid-July 2026 and has set no new date. The Financial
Times reported that eu-LISA, the agency building it, has internally accepted a
2026 launch is not feasible. The €20 fee and the under-18 and over-70 exemptions
are right; the date is not, and it was the date that would have shaped what an
agent told a customer booking for this winter.

Corrected across all 40, plus 5 Portugal records (FAO, LIS, OPO, FNC, PXO) and
2 Cyprus records (LCA, PFO) that are live to clients and carried the same claim.

Sources: europa.eu ETIAS pages via the House of Commons Library briefing
(commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10676), etias.com,
connaughtlaw.com, schengentraveler.com, traveltourister.com.

## The Greece-specific error

**All 20 Greek records said EES was fully operational with biometric scans on
first arrival.** The real position is more useful to an agent and more
complicated:

- EES went fully live at every Schengen external border on 10 April 2026.
- On that date Greece initially excluded British passport holders from biometric
  registration, to avoid queues at island airports.
- Greek authorities then clarified that a pause may only be applied at specific
  crossings during heavy traffic, and never to a single nationality. The blanket
  British exemption was withdrawn.
- On 7 July 2026 Greece and eight other countries asked the Commission to extend
  the pause mechanism beyond its **6 September 2026** expiry. As of 21 July the
  Commission had refused, holding that existing rules already allow enough
  flexibility.

So an island airport may or may not take biometrics on the day, and the
flexibility that allows that is due to lapse on 6 September. The records now say
to allow for biometric registration and treat a quick passage as a bonus.

Sources: euronews.com, connexionfrance.com, visaverge.com, etias.com,
blog.wego.com, travelandtourworld.com.

## Confirmed correct, no change

- **Spain**: EES has run at every Spanish border crossing without interruption
  since 10 April 2026. All 20 Spanish records were right about this.
- **Cyprus**: both records already said Cyprus is in the EU but not in Schengen,
  that EES does not apply and that passports are still stamped manually. Checked
  against the House of Commons Library briefing and cyprusvisa.co.uk. Correct.
  Only the ETIAS date needed removing.

## Still outstanding

Status `In progress`, so the Status gate keeps them out of the picker and off

No live record now carries the claim.
