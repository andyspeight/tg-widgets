## The verified entry block applied to all of them

Two-sourced 26 Aug 2026:
- No visa for stays up to 90 days in any 180 across the Schengen area.
- Passport issued less than 10 years before the date of entry AND valid at
  least 3 months after intended departure.
- **EES fully operational since 10 April 2026**: fingerprints and facial
  image on first entry, no passport stamp.
- **ETIAS is NOT live.** The EU withdrew its own late-2026 target in July 2026
  and has set no new date. Nothing to apply for or pay.
Sources: European Commission home-affairs + France Diplomatie (EES);
Fragomen + travel-europe.europa.eu / ABTA (ETIAS withdrawal).

## Errors found and corrected

**Vienna CAT is suspended.** The City Airport Train is not running as a train
from August 2026 until 31 October 2027. It is a premium bus replacement while
modernisation work happens. The record sold the 16-minute CAT ride. Rewritten
to point at the ÖBB Railjet/S7 at ~€4.40, which is cheaper and about as quick
anyway. This is live right now, not a future change.

**Munich S-Bahn fare** was €13.20. The airport is in outer MVV zone 5, so a
single covering zones M–5 is **around €15** as of mid-2026 (published figures
range €13.60–€15.10). Added the Airport City Day Ticket at ~€17.50 as better
value for more than one journey.

**Corrupted currency symbols** on Budapest and Warsaw taxi fares. Both showed
`¤` where `£` belongs. Fixed.

## Non-EU Schengen distinctions written in

Switzerland, Norway and Iceland are in Schengen but **not** the EU: EES applies
and ETIAS will, but customs allowances are non-EU. That matters for duty free,
and for Norway specifically it is worth using given alcohol prices.

Croatia joined Schengen **and** the euro in January 2023, so the kuna is gone.

Malta drives on the **LEFT**, same as the UK, the only Schengen country in
this set that does, and a real advantage for hire car customers.

## Airport traps recorded

- **Ryanair's "Düsseldorf" is often Weeze**, 70 km north-west.
