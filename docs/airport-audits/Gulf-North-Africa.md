# Gulf, Turkey and North Africa: the last 17 live records

Audited 25 August 2026: UAE (5), Turkey (5), Saudi Arabia (2), Egypt (2),
Morocco (2), Qatar (1). Prior Verified Date on every one: 11 May 2026.

Entry rules dominate this batch, so gov.uk FCDO travel advice was used as the
primary source throughout. It is the most authoritative source available for a
UK audience and it is what an agent would be held to.

## Errors corrected

| Country | What the records said | Verified position (FCDO) |
|---|---|---|
| **UAE** (all 5) | "No visa needed up to **30 days**" | UK passport holders get a free visit visa on arrival valid for up to **90 days in any 180-day period**. Our figure was a third of the real allowance |
| **Egypt** (both) | Visa on arrival **$25** | **$30**, cash only, bought at kiosks before the immigration counters |
| **Saudi Arabia** (both) | "e-Visa, USD 117 / £94, **single-entry valid 1 year**, 90 days each visit" | An **electronic travel authorisation valid 730 days**, **multiple entry**, capped at **180 days in total** across that period. The product and its terms had both changed |
| **Turkey** (all 5) | "Visa-free 90 days" | Correct, but incomplete. It is 90 days **in any 180-day period**, and Turkey requires **at least 150 days' passport validity** from arrival, which is stricter than the usual 3 or 6 months and is missed at check-in |

The UAE one is the most consequential. A customer told they have 30 days when
they actually have 90 has been given advice that could shorten a trip they were
entitled to take.

The Saudi fee of $117 / £94 could not be re-verified from an authoritative
source, so it has been removed rather than carried forward. The records now tell
agents to quote the current fee from the official portal.

## Confirmed correct, no change

- **Morocco** (AGA, RAK): visa-free up to 90 days, passport valid 3 months
  beyond arrival. Both records right on both points.
- **Egypt's free Sinai Stamp**: arriving by air into Sharm El-Sheikh and staying
  under 15 days in Sharm, Dahab, Nuweiba or Taba needs no visa. Right in both
  records, including the important distinction that Hurghada does not qualify.
- **EES does not apply** in any of these six countries. Right in all 17.

## Softened rather than guessed

**Qatar.** The records said 30 days visa-free on arrival. No FCDO page came back
in the search, and the other sources split between 30 days extendable by a
further 30 and up to 90 days. Rather than pick one, the record now states the
rule (no advance visa, admitted free on arrival), reports the disagreement and
tells the agent to confirm the current allowance before quoting it.

## Added

The FCDO's own warning for Egypt, that agents often charge more than the
official fee and customers should not buy a visa from one. That is worth an
agent knowing before a customer is talked into an overpriced service.
