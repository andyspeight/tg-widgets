# Australia, India, USA, Thailand, Canada, Ireland: 26 August 2026

32 records promoted to Done.

## Entry rules verified per country

**Australia (7)**: ETA subclass 601, **AUD 20**, applied for through the
official Australian ETA mobile app (not a paper form, not a third-party site).
Valid 12 months, stays up to three months per visit. Strict biosecurity:
declare all food, plant and animal products or risk an on-the-spot fine.
Drives on the LEFT.

**India (6)**: e-Visa from the official Government of India portal.
Fees vary sharply: ~US$25 for 30 days, ~US$40 for one year, but **~US$484 for
the five-year**, because India applies a reciprocal fee to UK nationals
specifically. That figure shocks people, so quote the variant that fits the
trip. Many lookalike sites charge large mark-ups. Drives on the LEFT.

**USA (6)**: ESTA **US$40**, up from US$21 on 30 September 2025, valid two
years, esta.cbp.dhs.gov only. Mobile Passport Control is free and saves real
time at CBP; Global Entry US$120 for five years. EES/ETIAS do not apply.

**Thailand (5)**: see below, this is the live one.

**Canada (4)**: eTA **CAD 7**, valid up to five years or until passport
expiry, official Canada.ca site only. Applies to **air arrivals only**; land
or sea entry does not need one.

**Ireland (4)**: Common Travel Area, **not Schengen**. No visa, no EES
biometric registration, and ETIAS will not apply. Airlines still require photo
ID. Euro, not sterling. Drives on the LEFT.

## The live one: Thailand

UK passport holders currently get **60 days visa-free**. The Thai cabinet
approved cutting that to **30 days on 19 May 2026**. It takes effect 15 days
after publication in the Royal Gazette, **which had not happened as of August
2026**, so 60 days still applies today.

Every visitor, including visa-exempt Britons, must submit the **Thailand
Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)** within 72 hours before arrival. Free, online,
replaced the paper TM6 card, and separate from the old Thailand Pass.

Both facts are written into all five Thai records with the caveat that the
60-day figure will change and must be re-checked before quoting a long stay.
Sources: two independent trackers confirming the cabinet approval and the
non-publication as of mid-August 2026.

## Operational detail worth having

- **Sydney has an 11pm–6am curfew**; Melbourne does not, which is why some
  long-haul prefers it.
- **Queensland does not observe daylight saving**, so in the Australian summer
  the Gold Coast runs an hour behind Sydney.
- **South Australia has its own fruit fly quarantine** on top of national
  biosecurity.
- **Cairns stinger season** runs roughly November to April, overlapping the wet
  season; red flags are not advisory.
- **Chiang Mai's burning season**, February to April, produces some of the
  worst air quality in the world, a real reason to steer customers elsewhere.
- **Koh Samui airport is owned by Bangkok Airways**, an effective monopoly, so
  the final leg costs far more than the distance suggests.
- **Dublin and Shannon both have US Border Preclearance**: passengers clear
  American immigration in Ireland and arrive in the States as domestic.
- **Goa now has two airports**: Dabolim and the newer Mopa in the north.
