# France — audit log, 26 August 2026

8 records: CDG ORY NCE LYS MRS BOD TLS BIQ. All promoted to Done.

## Errors found and corrected

**Paris airport rail fare, both airports.** Records said RER B to CDG was
€11.80 and Metro 14 to Orly €10.30. Île-de-France Mobilités has replaced
both with a single flat **"Paris Region ↔ Aéroports" ticket at €14**, which
also covers connecting metro and RER in zones 1–5 for two hours. Both
figures understated the cost, which is the direction that annoys a customer
at a ticket machine.
Sources: iledefrance-mobilites.fr / bonjour-ratp.fr (operator) + parisbytrain.com.

**Orly taxi flat fare.** Record said €41 Left Bank / €50 Right Bank. The
prefectural decree sets **€37 Left Bank / €44 Right Bank**. Note these are
the opposite way round to CDG, where the Left Bank is the dearer side —
easy to transpose.
Sources: g7.fr (Paris taxi operator) + allotaxi.net.

**CDG taxi flat fare confirmed correct** at €56 Right Bank / €65 Left Bank,
identical day or night, no luggage surcharge, up to four passengers.

## Verified and unchanged

- CDG: three terminals (T1, T2 A–G, T3), CDGVAL people-mover 8 minutes.
- ORY: Metro Line 14 direct since June 2024, ~25 minutes to Châtelet.
- NCE: Tramway Line 2 direct from inside T2, ~12 minutes to Place Garibaldi.
- LYS: Rhônexpress ~30 minutes to Part-Dieu, and a TGV station inside the airport.

## Applied to all 8

The Schengen entry block verified 26 Aug 2026: 90 days in any 180, passport
issued less than 10 years before entry and valid 3 months beyond departure,
EES fully operational since 10 April 2026, ETIAS not live with no date set.

Biarritz carries an extra note the others do not need: France and Spain are
both in Schengen, so a day trip to San Sebastián does not reset the 90-day
allowance.

## Judgement calls

Lyon's Rhônexpress is written as "around €17" rather than the record's
€17.10. The exact figure moves with annual revisions and a stale decimal
reads as more precise than it is; the point worth conveying is that it is
dear for the distance.
