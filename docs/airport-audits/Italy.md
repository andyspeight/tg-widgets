# Italy — audit log, 25 and 26 August 2026

10 records: FCO MXP LIN BGY CIA NAP VCE PSA CTA OLB. All promoted to Done.

## Errors found and corrected

**Fiumicino taxi flat fare** was €50 in the record. It is **€55** into the
Aurelian Walls, set by city ordinance, covering luggage and any number of
passengers. Also added the trap the record missed: take a *Comune di Roma*
taxi, because *Comune di Fiumicino* cabs run a separate higher flat fare of
around €60 for the same trip.

**Ciampino taxi flat fare** was €31. It is **€40**, and carries the same
licensing trap: use a taxi licensed to Roma Capitale, not to Ciampino, as
some Ciampino-licensed drivers refuse the flat fare and run the meter, which
is not legal on this route.

**Fiumicino terminal count.** The record claimed five terminals with T5
active in one field and closed in another — internally contradictory. Only
**T1 and T3** operate. T2 was demolished to expand T1; T5 has been closed
since 2024.

**Malpensa Express** was €13. It is **€15** adult, €7.50 child 4–13.
Journey times corrected to 37 minutes to Cadorna and 51 to Centrale.

**Malpensa taxi flat fare** was €104. It is **€114**, set by Lombardy
regional decree, raised 1 July 2024.

**Fiumicino 2025 passengers** was "around 49 million". It was **~51 million**,
the first year above 50.

**Venice ATVO coach** €10 → around €12. **Alilaguna** stated as €15–21 →
around €18.

rather than either asserting it or ignoring the project.

for the airport section, with the full line to San Cristoforo completed
12 October 2024.
