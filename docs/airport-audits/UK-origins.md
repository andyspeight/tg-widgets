# UK origin airports: full re-verification

All 24 UK origin records audited 25 August 2026 against the airport-spotlight
methodology. Prior Verified Date on every one: 12 or 13 May 2026.

Two independent sources per fact-bearing claim, at least one dated within 12
months. Where sources disagreed the claim was softened to a rule plus a range
with the month checked, not split down the middle and not guessed.

## The cross-cutting finding: liquids

This was the single most valuable check and it was missing or wrong on most
records. UK airports have diverged, and the rule no longer travels with the
customer.

**Containers up to two litres allowed** (CT scanners installed): Heathrow,
Gatwick, London City, Southend, Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Bournemouth,
Belfast International, Belfast City.

**100ml container limit still enforced**: Manchester, Stansted, Luton,
Liverpool, Newcastle, Glasgow, Glasgow Prestwick, Aberdeen, East Midlands,
Leeds Bradford, Cardiff, Exeter, Southampton, Cornwall Newquay.

**The part almost every record got wrong**: at the 100ml airports the clear
plastic bag is no longer required either, and liquids can stay inside the cabin
bag. The bag went away everywhere. Only the container size still differs.

Three pairings are worth flagging to agents because the two nearest airports
now run different rules: Bournemouth allows two litres while Southampton does
not, Bristol allows two litres while Cardiff does not, and Edinburgh allows two
litres while Glasgow and Prestwick do not.

Sources: which.co.uk, timeout.com/uk, expressandstar.com, travelgossip.co.uk,
nationalexecutivetransfers.co.uk, aylesburyairporttransfers.co.uk, plus each
airport's own security page where reachable.

## Confirmed correct, no change needed

Several records were in good order and are worth saying so. EDI, BHX and BRS
already carried the correct liquids position and current drop-off charges with
the right change dates. LTN's DART detail, including the booking trap where a
ticket to "Luton Airport Parkway" excludes the DART while one to "London Luton
Airport" includes it, checked out exactly. LCY's drop-off structure, introduced
6 January 2026 at £8 for five minutes then £1 a minute to a ten minute cap,
matched the airport's own announcement.

## Softened rather than guessed

- **MAN drop-off.** Three different price sets are in circulation and no two
  authoritative sources agree. The record now carries the mechanism, a range and
  the month checked.
- **LPL drop-off.** Sources give both around £6 for 10 minutes and from £3 for
  20 minutes. Stated as a range.
- **EDI tram fare.** Quoted between £5.50 and £8.50 across sources. Stated as a
  range with the day ticket figure.
- **Taxi fares generally.** Given as a range with the governing rule, metered or
  regulated, rather than a single figure. This is the change that removes the
  error class found at Malaga, where a metered fare with a minimum was
  described as fixed.

## Still open

- **BHX Premium Set Down at around £6 for 15 minutes** rests on one source.
  Nothing contradicts it, so it stands with the month checked, but a second
  source would settle it.
- **STN and Southend contactless.** Stansted Airport station also joined the
  contactless zone on 8 March 2026. Added to Southend, still to add to Stansted.
