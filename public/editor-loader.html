Matte colour

Transparent sits over any page. A matte colour gives clean edges on GIF, which only supports hard-cut transparency.

Label colour

Higher is smoother but makes a bigger file. 24 to 30 suits most loaders. GIF times its frames in hundredths of a second, so 50 is the ceiling.

GIF: blend soft shading against

Parts of a loader are drawn faintly — the globe fills itself at 8% and draws its grid lines at about a third. GIF has no half-transparency, so those are blended against this colour. Set it to the background of the page the GIF will sit on. WebM keeps them see-through and ignores this.

The download uses the current template, colours and the transparent or matte choice from the Design tab.

APNG, MP4 and PNG sequence are not built yet. GIF works everywhere — email, Duda, PowerPoint, Slack. WebM keeps soft edges and stays small as the loader gets bigger, but it needs a video tag on a web page. At small sizes GIF is often the smaller file.
