Special Offer Card

The display card for a hand-built offer, in two layouts. Vertical drops into an offers grid; horizontal suits a list or a banner row. Both read the same offer object the builder produces and match the existing offers widget, so hand-built cards sit beside your live cached deals. Clicking a card will open the offer page once that is built.
