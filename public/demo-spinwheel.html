A fun little nudge that turns a browsing visitor into an enquiry. Land on a destination, then enquire with one tap. Try it.

Why it works

People love a game. The wheel gives an undecided visitor a playful reason to pick a destination, and a warm prompt to get in touch while they are excited.

  • Weight the segments so popular trips come up more often
  • Land on a destination, a discount or a freebie
  • One spin per visitor option, remembered in their browser
  • Your colours, your call to action, no API key
