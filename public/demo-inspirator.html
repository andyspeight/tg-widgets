For the customer who cannot answer "where do you want to go?" but can answer "do you like the look of this?". They swipe through real destinations, keep the ones they like, and the agent receives a shortlist with the taste behind it rather than an email address and a blank box. Drag a card, use the buttons, or press the left and right arrow keys.
