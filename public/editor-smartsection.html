How to use it
Smart Section is a wrapper. Get the embed code, then put the content you want to show or hide inside the <div> — a banner, an offer, a whole section, even other Travelgenix widgets. Everything inside follows your rules. The preview here uses sample content; on your site it wraps whatever you put in it.
