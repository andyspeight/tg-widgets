How much spending money for a week in Spain?

It depends on your pace, but most couples we send to the Costa del Sol budget somewhere between sixty and ninety euros a day once the hotel and flights are paid for. That covers lunch out, a long dinner, a few drinks and the odd taxi.

The easiest way to plan is to decide your daily figure in pounds, then see what it buys you on the ground. Pop your number in below.

A few tips. Carry a little cash for tips and small cafes, but cards are fine almost everywhere. Always pay in the local currency when a card machine offers you the choice, the shop's own conversion is rarely in your favour. And tell your bank you are travelling so nothing gets blocked on day one.

If you are heading further afield, the same rule holds: know your daily number, know the rate, and you will never be surprised at the cashpoint.
