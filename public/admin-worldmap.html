Offers expire after 70 hours. Each refresh merges in fresh offers and removes any that are older than 70 hours or whose travel date has passed. A destination that stops returning offers from the supplier will empty out within 70 hours even if nothing replaces it. The Refresh every control above sets how often each destination is re-polled — it does not change this expiry window.
