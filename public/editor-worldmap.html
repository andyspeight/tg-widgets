Country codes of the departure airports to include, comma separated (GB = United Kingdom, IE = Ireland). Leave blank to show offers from all departure points.
Deal links
When on, clicking a deal searches that specific hotel on your booking site. When off, it searches the deal's destination with matching dates, board and rating (the current behaviour). Deals without a property reference always fall back to the destination search.
