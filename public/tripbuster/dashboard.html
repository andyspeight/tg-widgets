What you pay for

Some agencies sell online, some sell on the phone, and plenty do both. Choose what suits you. Any deal can be set differently in its own settings.

Your dashboard counts every phone enquiry. You are only charged for calls that connect and run past the length you set above, so wrong numbers and hang-ups cost you nothing. Until call tracking numbers are switched on we can see that someone pressed call, but not whether you answered, and those are never charged for.
Pricing is not finalised. The allowances shown are what the platform enforces right now. Upgrades and billing are not switched on yet, so get in touch if you need more live deals.
