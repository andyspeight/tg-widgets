Travelgenix Widget Suite

Destination Carousel

A cinematic showcase for your destinations. Cards enter small on the right, travel left growing through a fanned sweep, and arrive as a full-size hero with title, copy and call to action. Click a card, swipe, use the arrows — or just let it play.

Embed it on your site

Create and style your carousel in the editor, then paste two lines anywhere on your page:

<div data-tg-widget="carousel" data-tg-id="YOUR_WIDGET_ID"></div>
<script src="https://tg-widgets.vercel.app/widget-carousel.js"></script>

The widget is self-contained: Shadow DOM keeps its styles isolated from your site, it adapts to the width of its container (collapsing to a single full-bleed slide below 720px), respects reduced-motion preferences, and pauses its auto-rotation while off-screen.
